About

From political science to production code.

I'm a self-taught developer with a degree in Political Science and Classics from the University of Ghana. I found my way into tech through data work, and stayed because I love building things that solve real problems.

Today I design and build websites and web applications for clients under my studio, WebNest, working mostly with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma and Postgres.
